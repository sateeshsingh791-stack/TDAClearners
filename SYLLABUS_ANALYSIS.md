#### Extracted Course Structure:

1. **`BVTD111` — Design Foundation & Basics of Textile**
   - **Category**: Major Course (Theory)
   - **Contact Hours/Week**: 1 Hr | **Credits**: 2 (L:2, T:0, P:0)
   - **Marks Breakdown**: Theory: 37 | Practical: -- | Internal Assessment (IA): 13 | **Total**: 50
   - **Syllabus Page Ref**: Page 10
   - **Overview**: Fundamental concepts of textile design, fibre origins, polymer structures, and basic textile classification.

2. **`BVTD112` — Design Foundation & Basics of Textile (Practical)**
   - **Category**: Major Course (Practical)
   - **Contact Hours/Week**: 4 Hrs | **Credits**: 2 (L:0, T:0, P:2)
   - **Marks Breakdown**: Theory: -- | Practical: 37 | Internal Assessment (IA): 13 | **Total**: 50
   - **Syllabus Page Ref**: Page 11
   - **Overview**: Laboratory experiments on fibre identification (microscopic & burning tests) and textile design swatching.

3. **`BVTD113` — Sewing Techniques (Practical)**
   - **Category**: Major Course (Practical)
   - **Contact Hours/Week**: 8 Hrs | **Credits**: 4 (L:0, T:0, P:4)
   - **Marks Breakdown**: Theory: -- | Practical: 75 | Internal Assessment (IA): 25 | **Total**: 100
   - **Syllabus Page Ref**: Page 11 & 12
   - **Course Objectives**:
     - To impart knowledge of sewing techniques.
     - To apply the knowledge for basic stitching.
   - **Section-I Syllabus Topics**:
     - Introduction to different parts of sewing machine and their care and maintenance.
     - Basic hand sewing techniques.
     - Seams and seam finishes: plain, run and fell, French, counter.
     - Fullness: yoke with fullness, gathers, darts, pleats.
     - Plackets: French placket, continuous, extended placket.
     - Pockets: patch, welt, in seam, kurta.
     - Sleeves: plain, puff, bishop, bell, cap, raglan, kimono and dolman.
     - Collars: flat Peter Pan collar, shawl, mandarin, cape collar.
     - Design any dress by using above fashion details (Kids).
   - **Books Prescribed**:
     1. *The Art of couture sewing* by Zoya Nudelman (Bloomsbury Academic)
     2. *The sewing Book* by Alison Smith
     3. *Sewing Techniques and Patterns* by Marie-Naelle Bayard (Sterling)
     4. *Complete Guide to Sewing* by Reader's Digest
     5. *The ultimate sewing book* by Maggi McCormick (Garden Collins & Brown)
   - **Course Outcomes**:
     - COS1: Explain about the various components of garment construction and its application.
     - COS2: To gain knowledge in seams and seam finishes.
     - COS3: To enable the students a basic garment construction details.

4. **`BVTD114` — Introduction to Entrepreneurship**
   - **Category**: Minor Course (Theory)
   - **Contact Hours/Week**: 4 Hrs | **Credits**: 4 (L:4, T:0, P:0)
   - **Marks Breakdown**: Theory: 75 | Practical: -- | Internal Assessment (IA): 25 | **Total**: 100 (Paper max 50 for theory option: Th 37 + IA 13)
   - **Syllabus Page Ref**: Page 13-14
   - **Course Objectives**: To impart the knowledge of Entrepreneurship - which will help the students to start their enterprise.
   - **Units & Topics**:
     - **Unit-I**: Entrepreneurship — Concept, functions and need.
     - **Unit-II**: Characteristics of entrepreneurship — Process of entrepreneurship development.
     - **Unit-III**: Help and support to entrepreneurs.
     - **Unit-IV**: Barriers to entrepreneurship.
   - **Books Prescribed**:
     1. *Entrepreneurial Development* by Dr. S. Moharana and Dr. Dash (RBSA Publishers, Jaipur)
     2. *Entrepreneurial Development* by S.S. Khanna (S. Chand & Company Ltd.)
     3. *Entrepreneurial Development* by C.B. Gupta and N.P. Srinivasan (Sultan Chand & Co.)
